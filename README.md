# Hi, I'm Andrin 👋

I'm a master student at the University of Zurich interested in physics, neuroinformatics and machine learning. I enjoy using code and mathematical models to explore complex systems.

## Research experience

For my bachelor’s thesis, I worked with probabilistic models of lymphatic tumour spread, focusing on hypopharyngeal cancer. My work involved fitting and evaluating models against clinical data.

Along the way, I gained practical experience with:

* **Python** for scientific computing, data analysis and visualisation
* **Hidden Markov models** for modelling disease progression
* **Bayesian inference** for parameter estimation and uncertainty quantification
* **Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)** sampling with `emcee`
* **HDF5** for storing and analysing sampling results
* Comparing model predictions with observed patterns in clinical data

## Projects and tools

During my BSc thesis on probabilistic modelling of lymphatic spread in hypopharyngeal cancer, I used the following repositories:

* [lyscripts](https://github.com/lycosystem/lyscripts) and [lynference](https://github.com/lycosystem/lynference): Research software supporting the modelling and analysis undertaken in my thesis.
* [oral-cavity-model-paper](https://github.com/rmnldwg/oral-cavity-model-paper): Reference analyses used to compare my findings with modelling results for oral cavity cancer.
